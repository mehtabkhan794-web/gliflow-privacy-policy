📜 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Mehtab Labs ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Gliflow.

Gliflow is a creative text utility application that provides stylish fonts, ASCII art generation, Kaomojis, Unicode symbols, and text design tools.

🌍 Multi-Store Availability

Gliflow is available through multiple marketplaces including:

  • Google Play Store
  • Amazon Appstore

Certain features such as subscriptions, advertising, and billing may vary depending on the platform used.

🔒 Information We Collect

We believe in privacy-first design. Gliflow does not collect, upload, transmit, or store your text creations, generated fonts, ASCII art, Kaomojis, Unicode symbols, or personal content.

We may collect limited anonymous information such as:

  • Crash reports
  • Performance diagnostics
  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Device and OS information

💻 Local Processing & No Server Storage

All text processing, font generation, ASCII art creation, Kaomoji formatting, and Unicode conversion happen entirely on your device.

We do not store user content on our servers.

Your generated content remains under your control unless you explicitly choose to share it using other apps or services.

⭐ Gliflow Pro & Premium Content

Gliflow follows a strict premium model.

Approximately 70% of content, tools, and advanced resources are reserved exclusively for Gliflow Pro members.

Free users have access to approximately 30% of the available core content and features.

💳 Payments & Billing

Subscription and premium purchases are securely processed through:

  • Google Play Billing
  • Amazon In-App Purchasing (IAP)

We do not collect, process, access, or store:

  • Credit card details
  • Debit card information
  • Bank account details
  • Financial credentials
  • Payment authentication data

All payments are handled directly by Google or Amazon under their respective policies.

📢 Advertising

Gliflow may display advertisements through Google AdMob on devices and platforms that support Google Play Services.

On platforms where Google Play Services are unavailable, such as certain Amazon devices, advertisements may be limited or disabled.

Advertising providers may collect advertising identifiers and device information according to their own privacy policies.

📋 Permissions

Gliflow may request the following permissions:

  • Storage Access – To save generated content.
  • Clipboard Access – To copy and paste user-generated content.

These permissions are used only when initiated by the user and are never used to collect personal information.

📊 Analytics & Crash Reporting

To improve reliability and performance, we may use analytics and crash reporting services that collect anonymous technical information.

  • Application version
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Crash logs
  • Performance metrics

These services do not provide us with the text content created within Gliflow.

👶 Children's Privacy

Gliflow is not specifically directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

🛡️ Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed by the App.

However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

🔗 Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob

  • Amazon In-App Purchasing (IAP)
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting Services

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

🌐 International Users

Gliflow is available worldwide. Users acknowledge that certain technical data may be processed by third-party providers located in different countries.
